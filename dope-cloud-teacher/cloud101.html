Try the teaching approach

Before you create anything, ask these three questions.

Who can access it?

Check your permissions. Give people and apps only the access needed for their task. Protect your sign-in with multi-factor authentication.

What could it cost?

Check the provider’s pricing for the service and region you choose. A budget alert helps you monitor spending; it is not automatically a spending cap.

How will I clean it up?

Keep a list of the resources you create. Follow the lab cleanup instructions, including related storage or network resources that may keep generating charges.
