About The Dope Cloud Teacher

The Dope Cloud Teacher is a Prince George's County technology education company that teaches cloud computing, AI, and cybersecurity to youth, career changers, seniors, and workforce learners across the DMV — and pairs every technical skill with the governance, compliance, and human oversight needed to use it responsibly.

Our mission is simple: technology only helps people when someone understands how to govern it. We do not stop at teaching a tool. We teach who is accountable for it, what it should never be allowed to touch, and how a team keeps a human in the loop when it matters. That applies whether we're in a classroom, a senior center, or a government training room.
