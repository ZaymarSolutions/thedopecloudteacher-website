The DCT standard

More than technical skills.

We do not just teach people how to use technology. We teach them how to use it well, safely, responsibly, and with integrity.

Every DCT instructor is expected to model sound judgment, patience, professionalism, digital citizenship, security and privacy awareness, responsible AI use, source verification, bias awareness, human oversight, and respect for every learner.
