PGCPS · AI Governance

PGCPS is formalizing guardrails for staff AI use.

PGCPS directs staff to use vetted AI tools, protect personally identifiable and confidential information, and review AI output for accuracy and appropriateness. Student access to large language models remains limited to approved pilots.

Why DCT cares: Responsible AI is now a practical workforce skill. Knowing how to prompt is not enough; learners also need privacy, verification, bias awareness and human accountability.

PGCPS · Procurement

AI-enabled school tools face explicit vetting requirements.

PGCPS vendor criteria require disclosure when products use AI, machine learning or automated decision-making, including underlying models, intended users and data captured for AI functionality.

What partners should do: Treat AI governance as part of program design and procurement from the beginning—not a disclaimer added at the end.

The DCT AI Guardrails

These are DCT's practical teaching guardrails, informed by current public-sector AI guidance and established security habits. They are not a claim of PGCPS endorsement.

1 · Use the right toolChoose an approved, appropriate tool for the task.
2 · Minimize the dataShare only what is necessary. Keep PII, confidential and protected data out.
3 · Verify the outputAI can be confidently wrong. Check important claims against reliable sources.
4 · Check bias & contextAsk whose perspective is missing and whether the result is fair and appropriate.
5 · Keep a human accountablePeople—not the model—own decisions, approvals and consequences.
6 · Protect accessUse sound identity, password/passkey, device and account-security practices.
7 · Be transparentDisclose meaningful AI assistance when policy, school, work or context requires it.
8 · Escalate uncertaintyWhen stakes are high or policy is unclear, stop and ask before acting.
