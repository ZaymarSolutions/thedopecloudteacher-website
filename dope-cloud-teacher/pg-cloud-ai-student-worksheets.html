Cloud & AI Fundamentals Student Workbook

Student Workbook for the 6-Module Flagship Course

This workbook follows the exact academy build plan: six modules, hands-on labs, guided reflection, and a capstone choice at the end. Print it or use it digitally during class.

6 modulesCloud, portal, labs, AI, security, and capstone planning.
5 lab momentsPortal navigation, first VM, AI assistant, secure environment, capstone build.
Portfolio-readyDesigned to produce evidence students can show after class.

Workbook Use

  • Open before each class and complete the note prompts.
  • Save screenshots, links, and reflection notes in one place.
  • Use the lab checklists to track progress through each module.
  • Finish by choosing one capstone outcome to build and present.
