From curiosity to a clear next step.

1. Choose your starting point

Tell us your track, goals, and preferred schedule. No previous cloud experience is needed for fundamentals.

2. Review a confirmed offer

Receive actual dates, instructor, format, price, prerequisites, accessibility options, and cancellation terms before enrolling.

3. Enroll and get ready

Once registration opens, complete the stated registration process and receive confirmation, class access, and a preparation checklist. County classes use the county’s own registration.

4. Build, assess, and move forward

Complete guided practice, a practical project, and a readiness review. Leave with a study plan or next-level class recommendation.
