How Class Flow Works

Simple version: choose your path, learn one topic at a time, practice with guided tasks, then prove your progress with quizzes and mini projects.

Step 1: Choose Your Lane

Pick in-person, live virtual, or self-paced academy based on your schedule. The curriculum is shared, so you can switch lanes without starting over.

Step 2: Learn in Plain Language

Every lesson explains the concept in everyday terms first, then introduces technical wording so beginners are not left behind.

Step 3: Practice a Real Scenario

You complete a practical scenario like locking down access, planning a cloud deployment, or troubleshooting a monitoring alert.

Step 4: Validate and Level Up

Quizzes, playbook visuals, and dashboard progress checks help you confirm what you know before moving to the next module.
