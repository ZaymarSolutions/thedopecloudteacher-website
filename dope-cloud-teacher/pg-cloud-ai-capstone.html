Cloud & AI Fundamentals Capstone

Choose One Build, Finish Strong, and Present a Real Outcome

The capstone is the final production step in the course. Students choose one outcome, scope it clearly, and leave with something they can explain, show, and improve after class.

View Capstone Choices Open Workbook

Success Criteria

  • One focused problem or use case
  • One clear build path
  • One evidence artifact or demo
  • One short presentation story

Capstone Choices

Portfolio

Personal Portfolio

Build a polished personal site that highlights skills, labs, and learning outcomes.

Business

Business Website

Create a simple business site with a clear offer, contact path, and service description.

AI Assistant

AI Business Assistant

Design a prompt-driven assistant that helps with customer support, content, or operations.

Cloud

Cloud File Server

Organize and secure files in a cloud-based structure that demonstrates storage and access control.

Career

AI Resume Builder

Use AI and cloud tools to generate a resume workflow that improves job search readiness.

Capstone Workflow

1. ChoosePick one outcome and write the goal in one sentence.
2. ScopeList the minimum features needed to finish well.
3. BuildWork in class on the highest-value part first.
4. PresentShare what was built, why it matters, and what comes next.

Instructor Notes

  • Keep the capstone small enough for a first successful completion.
  • Encourage students to reuse work from earlier modules where possible.
  • Use the workbook reflection prompts to document progress and confidence.
  • Close the course with a final review and a next-step recommendation.
